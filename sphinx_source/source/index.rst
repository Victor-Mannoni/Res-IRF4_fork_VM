Res-IRF4
========

.. image:: https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.6553021.svg
   :target: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6553021
   :alt: DOI

The **Res-IRF** model is a tool for simulating energy consumption and energy efficiency improvements in the French 
residential building sector. It currently focuses on **space heating** as the main usage. The rationale for its 
development is to integrate a detailed description of the energy performance of the dwelling stock with a rich 
description of household behaviour. Res-IRF has been developed to improve the behavioural realism that is typically 
lacking in integrated models of energy demand.

About the authors
-----------------

The development of the Res-IRF model was initiated at **CIRED** in 2008. Coordinated by *Louis-Gaëtan Giraudet*, it involved,
over the years (in alphabetical order), *Louise Asselin, Cyril Bourgeois, Frédéric Branger, François Chabrol, 
David Glotin, Céline Guivarch, Philippe Quirion, and Lucas Vivier*.
